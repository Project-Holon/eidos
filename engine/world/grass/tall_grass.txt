# Normal tall grass: every leaf drawn by hand.
#
# The game's two sprites (kusa_ec1 the light top, kusa_ec2 the dark body) show the plant from above: a picture of
# leaves lying.  A leaf stood up from that picture shows a top view from the side, in pixels as large as the
# ground's.  So here each of the plant's thirteen leaves is drawn again, pixel by pixel, as that leaf seen flat on
# (its tip up, its root down), in the sprite's own colours and with the sprite's own marks: its outline colour, its
# lit streak where the sprite has it, its dark tip.  A drawn pixel is 0.467 map units (a little larger than the
# pixels of the battles' grass tufts, 0.42, and close to a Pokemon's): the bush stands 6.3 map units tall.
#
# Compass: yaw 0 east, 90 south (toward the field's camera), 180 west, 270 north.  A tile is 16 map units.
